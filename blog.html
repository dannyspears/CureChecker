One new post a day on the biggest update in the research we track

In short: Every day we look through all the conditions and diseases on Cure Checker for new studies, approvals and trial news, then write up the most important update in plain language, with sources.

COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease) · October 5, 2026

COPD: Tozorakimab Cut Flare-Ups in Two Phase 3 Trials

Tozorakimab cut COPD flare-ups by 29% and 34% in former smokers in two Phase 3 trials of 2,306 adults. What was reported and what comes next.

Crohn's disease · October 5, 2026

Crohn's Disease: Guselkumab Five-Year GALAXI 1 Results

Five-year GALAXI 1 data on guselkumab (Tremfya) in Crohn's disease: 97.7% of those still on it were in remission, but 46.0% of all who started.

HIV · October 5, 2026

FDA Approves Bixlenvo, a Daily HIV Pill for Suppressed Adults

The FDA approved Bixlenvo on August 27, 2026, a once-daily pill that manages HIV in adults whose virus is already suppressed. It is not a cure.

Lupus (SLE) · October 5, 2026

Litifilimab for Skin Lupus: 52-Week Phase 2 Data Reported

Biogen reported 52-week Phase 2 data for litifilimab in cutaneous (skin) lupus: 27.2% reached clear or almost clear skin. Company-reported, not a cure.

Multiple sclerosis (MS) · October 5, 2026

MS Stem-Cell Transplant: Blood Markers Stay Low for Years

In 24 people with severe MS, inflammation markers stayed low for up to 5 years after a stem-cell transplant (aHSCT). What it shows and what it does not.

Osteosarcoma · October 5, 2026

Osteosarcoma: OST-HER2 Reports 71.2% Three-Year Survival

OS Therapies reports 71.2% three-year survival with OST-HER2 in lung-spread osteosarcoma, versus 45.8% in historical controls. Early, not randomized.

Pancreatic cancer · October 5, 2026

Pancreatic Cancer: VCN-01 Virus Therapy Phase 2b Results

VIRAGE, a Phase 2b trial in 112 people with metastatic pancreatic cancer, reported better survival when VCN-01 was added to chemo. What it shows.

Parkinson's disease · October 5, 2026

Rexaceract in Parkinson's: Small Phase 1b Extension Shows Promise

Gain Therapeutics reports stable motor scores over 12 months in a small, open-label Phase 1b extension of rexaceract for Parkinson's disease.

Spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) · October 5, 2026

SMA: FDA Approves Isembyld, a Muscle-Targeted Medicine

The FDA approved Isembyld (apitegromab) on Sept 11, 2026 for SMA in people 2 and older already on SMN2 treatment. Trial results and the fracture warning.

Type 1 diabetes · October 5, 2026

FDA Approves Finerenone for Kidney Disease in Type 1 Diabetes

On Sept 17, 2026 the FDA approved finerenone (Kerendia) for kidney disease in adults with type 1 diabetes. It does not treat diabetes itself.

ALS (Lou Gehrig's disease) · October 5, 2026

Ulefnersen Wins a Phase 3 Trial in FUS-ALS: What the Results Mean

Ulefnersen met its main goal in the Phase 3 FUSION trial for FUS-ALS (p = 0.0005). What was tested, what was reported, and what happens next.
