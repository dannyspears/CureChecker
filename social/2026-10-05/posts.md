## Post 2: Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)

Image: post-2.png

**Facebook**

Duchenne muscular dystrophy: what's new

The FDA extended its review of deramiocel, a cell therapy for Duchenne, to November 22, 2026, after an advisory panel voted 9–3 against it in July. Decisions on two exon-skipping drugs are due in early 2027.

Latest study we logged: Elevidys in ages 8–12: two-year data at World Muscle Society (Sep 30, 2026): Sarepta reported that 25 walking children treated at ages 8 to 12 did better on the North Star score (3.32 points) and on rising from the floor and walk/run speed over 2 years than 99 matched children who were not treated.

## Post 3: Type 1 diabetes

Image: post-3.png

**Facebook**

Type 1 diabetes: what's new

On Sept 17, the FDA approved finerenone (Kerendia) for kidney disease in adults with type 1 diabetes, the first new option in over 30 years (it treats kidney damage, not diabetes itself). A new analysis of the PROTECT trial (Diabetologia, Sep 10) backs teplizumab's June 2026 FDA approval for slowing insulin loss in newly diagnosed children 8 to 17. Vertex says new timelines for its zimislecel cell therapy will come later this year.

Latest study we logged: Rare severe reaction after teplizumab: case report (Oct 1, 2026): A 44-year-old man with Down syndrome developed a severe immune reaction (HLH) linked to Epstein-Barr virus reactivation after a teplizumab course.

Pancreatic cancer: what's new

FDA approves daraxonrasib (Rasonque), a first-of-its-kind pill that blocks many forms of RAS, the gene behind 9 in 10 pancreatic cancers (Aug 26). In its Phase 3 trial, median survival was 13.2 months vs 6.7 on chemo.

Latest study we logged: VCN-01 virus therapy + chemo (VIRAGE, Phase 2b, Nature Medicine) (Sep 30, 2026): In 112 people with newly diagnosed metastatic pancreatic cancer at 14 sites in the U.S.
