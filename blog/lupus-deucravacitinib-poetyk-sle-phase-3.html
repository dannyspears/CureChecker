Lupus: Deucravacitinib Phase 3 Trials Give Mixed Results

In short: On November 25, 2026, Bristol Myers Squibb reported that one of two Phase 3 trials of deucravacitinib (Sotyktu) in systemic lupus met its main goal and the other did not. The results are company-reported. The drug is not approved for lupus, and lupus has no cure.

What was announced

Bristol Myers Squibb announced results from two Phase 3 trials, POETYK SLE-1 and POETYK SLE-2, testing deucravacitinib (brand name Sotyktu) in adults with systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE).

The drug is already approved for plaque psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis. It is not approved for lupus.

What the two trials found

  • POETYK SLE-1 met its main goal. A significantly larger share of people taking deucravacitinib than placebo had an SRI-4 response at week 52. SRI-4 is a score that measures improvement in lupus disease activity.
  • POETYK SLE-2 did not meet its main goal. The company says people on placebo responded more than they did in SLE-1.
  • The company says key secondary measures in SLE-2 were numerically better than placebo.

What is not known yet

The announcement did not give response rates, so the size of the benefit is unknown. The results come from a company press release and have not been peer reviewed.

The company says detailed results will be shared at a future medical meeting, and that it will review all its lupus data and talk with health authorities.

Safety

The company says deucravacitinib was well tolerated in both trials, with a safety profile consistent with its use in psoriasis, and that few people stopped because of side effects. Detailed safety data have not been published.

What it means

One positive and one negative trial is a mixed result. Whether regulators would consider it enough is not known. Lupus still has no cure, and people with lupus should talk with their care team about treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Is deucravacitinib approved for lupus?

No. It is approved for plaque psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis. Its lupus trials were reported on November 25, 2026, and the company has not yet said whether it will file for approval.

Did the lupus Phase 3 trials of Sotyktu work?

One of two did by the company's report. POETYK SLE-1 met its main goal and POETYK SLE-2 did not. Detailed numbers have not been released.

Is there a cure for lupus?

No. Current treatments aim to control the disease. This news is about one possible treatment, not a cure.
