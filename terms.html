1. About Cure Checker

Cure Checker is an independent, informational website. Its purpose is to collect the most recent publicly available information about specific conditions/diseases in one place: research findings, clinical trial results, regulatory decisions and treatment developments. We present it in plain language for patients, families, caregivers and anyone else following the search for a cure.

By using this website, you agree to these Terms of Use. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

2. How the content is produced

Cure Checker's content is created with the help of artificial intelligence (AI). Automated AI tools gather information from publicly available sources, summarize it, and update the condition/disease reports. Reports on incurable diseases are updated every morning, and reports on other conditions are updated once a week. These sources include clinical trial registries, peer-reviewed journals, regulatory agencies, company announcements, news outlets and patient organizations. Some sections are retrieved in real time from public databases each time a page is opened. These include ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, Europe PMC, the ISRCTN registry and Drugs@FDA (via openFDA). Data from the EU Clinical Trials Information System (CTIS) is refreshed as a daily snapshot. Each report also links to the WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP), the Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials, the European Medicines Agency, OpenMD and, for cancers, the National Cancer Institute.

Counts and listings from these sources reflect how each database indexes and searches its records. They may include studies only loosely related to a condition/disease, miss some that are relevant, or differ between registries for the same trial.

AI can make mistakes. It may misread a source, leave out important context, oversimplify complex findings, or present information that has since changed. Source links are provided so you can read the original material and judge it for yourself.
